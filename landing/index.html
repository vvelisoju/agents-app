How it works

From a sentence to a live agent in three steps

No prompt engineering. No developers. No week-long setup. If you can describe your business, you can launch an agent.

Describe it

Say what your agent should do in plain words. Our builder drafts its name, personality and knowledge — you review and edit anything.

Train it

Paste your website link and add facts, FAQs and price lists. The agent uses them instantly — every edit applies to the very next message.

Launch it

Scan one QR to connect WhatsApp, test it in a private console, and go live. It now replies to every customer, day and night.

Agentic, not just a chatbot

It doesn’t just reply. It gets things done.

Approval before it acts

Anything that sends money, messages or writes data waits for your one-tap approval. Nothing runs behind your back.

You see everything it did

Every reply and every tool call is traced and audited, with per-account cost caps so spend never surprises you.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code? +

No. You describe what your agent should do in plain language, review the draft it builds, and launch. There’s no code and no training day.

Which channels are supported? +

WhatsApp is live today via a simple QR connection. Website chat widget, Instagram, Telegram and Email are on the roadmap — all on the same one-tap framework.

Can the agent actually do things, not just chat? +

Yes. Secure Agents is agentic: it can capture leads, book appointments, send follow-ups and monitor pages. Any action that writes or sends is approval-gated, so nothing happens without your permission.

How does it learn about my business? +

Paste your website link and add a few facts, FAQs and shareable links. The agent uses them instantly — edits apply to the very next message, with no retraining.

Is my data safe? +

Yes. Every account is isolated, secrets are encrypted at rest, sensitive actions are audited, and per-account cost caps prevent runaway spend. It’s built enterprise-grade from day one.
